# Lonecat's H3 Showrunner 🎬

Make a short movie with MiniMax H3 in ComfyUI. Write the story on one node (who is in it, where it happens, every shot), give each person and place a picture, and press **Run**. Showrunner renders the shots one after another and joins them into one movie with sound and dialogue.

- 📘 **[User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md)**: every part of the workflow, step by step, with screenshots.
- ✍️ **[Prompting Guide](PROMPTING_GUIDE.md)**: how to word looks, outfits, actions and lines so H3 makes the movie you meant.

**Needs:** ComfyUI_LC_AV_nodes, ComfyUI_LC123_nodes, comfyui-krea2edit, ComfyUI-KJNodes, ComfyUI-MiniMax-H3-Turbo, ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Director (the live preview), ComfyLiterals and ComfyUI_LC_MaskMaker_nodes. ComfyUI_LC_Vision_nodes is optional. The workflow is **MMH3 Showrunner** in the LC AV pack's `workflows` folder.

💡 Inside ComfyUI, **Vision Assistant → Tour** walks you through the workflow in 13 steps (English, 中文, 日本語, 한국어).
